About

I turn abstract problems into software that holds up, and I like it best when the problem comes from science.

I started at the Electrical Engineering High School in Mostar, writing C++, C# and Java. My graduation thesis was a web application for analyzing genetic code and modeling proteins, and it set the direction for what came next.

Today I study Software Engineering at the Faculty of Information Technologies in Mostar. In 2026 I spent an Erasmus+ semester at the University of Maribor, taking non-relational databases, information security, computer graphics and project management.

Outside coursework I solve Rosalind bioinformatics problems, model epidemics with differential equations and analyze RNA-seq data in R. Next I want a role in software or data engineering, followed by a master's degree with a scientific focus.
